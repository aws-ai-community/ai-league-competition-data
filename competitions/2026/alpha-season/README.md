# Agentic Football Alpha Season 2026

## Event Details

- **Event**: Agentic Football Alpha Season, a public league season run at agenticfootballcup.ai (separate from the AWS AI League knockout events)
- **Date**: September–October 2026 (7 weeks of competition)
- **Website**: [agenticfootballcup.ai](https://agenticfootballcup.ai/)
- **Format**: Six leagues (A to F) played as a season rather than a knockout bracket. Teams played about 70 matches each, as shown in the league tables below, and were ranked by points.
- **Game**: Agentic Football — build a team of AI agents that compete in live matches.
- **Prizes**: One winner per league, plus four special prizes (Fastest Goal, Winning Streak, Comeback of the Weekend, Giant Killer).

## Notes

### A note on the Winning Streak prize

The Winning Streak prize was first won by **Atlas Knights** (Mark Ross). Because he had already qualified through the Agentic Maze in the AI League, the prize was passed on to the next team, **Chrome Ballistas**, which belongs to DeepRacer favourite **Polish Thunder** (aka **Daryl Jezierski**).

Chrome Ballistas then finished top of League D on 2,297 points. As they had already taken the Winning Streak prize, the League D prize was passed on to the next team, **Chrome Birches** (2,262 points). That is why Chrome Birches are listed as the League D winner above, while Chrome Ballistas head the League D table below.

We understand that the same happened with at least one other special prize, but we do not have the details.

### A note on League A

Bugle Summits finished top of the League A table on 2,361 points, but the League A prize is listed as going to **Blissful Bandit** (2nd, 2,057 points). The reason is not known. It could be that Bugle Summits did not take up the prize or had already qualified for the finals by another route, but that has not been confirmed.

### A note on integrity concerns

Potential cheating was reported during the season by a number of competitors who noticed suspicious activity. Some contributors suggested "match fixing", for example one entrant registering multiple teams and playing against themselves to maximise wins with one strong team and one weak team. It also appears that some very high scoring entrants were removed from the leagues after the competition ended.

These are reports and suggestions only. They have not been confirmed, and no team or person is accused here. The standings in this write-up reflect the leaderboard as supplied.
